Before treating a finding as exploitable: confirm scanner coverage, affected assets, and deployment exposure. AI guidance is advisory; a PR is not evidence of closure.

How to work this queue
  1. Confirm the scanner ran. Incomplete coverage is not a clean bill of health.
  2. Investigate the exposure. An affected package or CVE does not prove exploitability in your deployment.
  3. Review the plan. AI suggestions are advisory; verify vendor fixes, tests and rollback.
  4. Implement with approval. Dependency upgrades and risky changes are not unattended.
  5. Rescan and verify. A merged pull request does not automatically close an issue.
